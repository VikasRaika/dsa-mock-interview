# Databricks DSA mock interview: Build, Demo, Pitch

This runs like the real thing. **Each stage is released only when you finish the one before it**, the same way the
real process hands you material at fixed points. Claude holds the later stages and releases them on cue.

| Stage | What happens | Released when |
|---|---|---|
| **Step 0: Prep** | Get your environment working and learn the concepts. No clock. | **Now** (`step0_prep/`) |
| Step 1: Prompt | The candidate prompt arrives (the "24 hours before your slot" email). | You report Step 0 done and pick a mock interview slot |
| Step 2: Drive access | The project scaffold (.zip) to deploy with DABs. Your 4-hour build clock starts. | You say "start the clock" |
| Step 3: The call | A 60-minute mock customer call with the COO, CTO and VP of Engineering, played by Claude. | You say "start the panel" |
| Step 4: Debrief | Score against the rubric, then the sealed answer key and persona notes are revealed. | After the call |

Rules (same as the real round): no more than 4 hours of build once Step 2 is released, everything must run in
Databricks Free Edition, and no extensions for tooling problems. That's why Step 0 exists.
